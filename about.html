Our Story

Discover the passion and dedication behind Travel Smart

How It All Began

Founded in 2010, Travel Smart began as a small team of travel enthusiasts who believed that exploring the world should be accessible, enjoyable, and transformative for everyone. What started as a passion project among friends has now grown into a leading travel platform serving thousands of travelers worldwide.

Our journey began when our founders, Sarah and Michael, encountered difficulties planning their dream vacation. They realized that while there was plenty of travel information available, it was scattered, overwhelming, and often unreliable. This inspired them to create a platform that would simplify travel planning while delivering exceptional value.

Today, we're proud to have helped over 50,000 travelers create unforgettable memories in more than 100 countries around the globe. Our mission remains the same: to make world-class travel experiences accessible to everyone.
