Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our travel services.

How far in advance should I book my trip?

We recommend booking at least 2-3 months in advance for domestic trips and 4-6 months for international travel. This ensures better availability and often lower prices for flights and accommodations.

What is your cancellation policy?

Our cancellation policy varies depending on the type of booking. Generally, we offer full refunds for cancellations made at least 30 days before the departure date. For cancellations within 30 days, fees may apply. Please refer to your specific booking terms for details.

Do you offer travel insurance?

Yes, we strongly recommend travel insurance for all trips. We offer comprehensive travel insurance plans that cover trip cancellations, medical emergencies, lost luggage, and other unforeseen circumstances. Our agents can help you choose the right plan for your needs.

Can you accommodate special dietary requirements?

Absolutely! We work with our partners to accommodate various dietary needs including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and allergies. Please inform us of any dietary restrictions at the time of booking so we can make the necessary arrangements.

Do you offer customized itineraries?

Yes, we specialize in creating personalized travel experiences. Our travel experts will work with you to design a custom itinerary based on your interests, budget, and preferences. Contact us to start planning your dream vacation.
